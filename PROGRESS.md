## Where things stand

- **Done:** the skeleton (Session 1), the quality system (Session 2), the forge (Session 3) and
  the first half of the polish (Session 4): the advancement trail, the Jade overlay, the mod
  icon, 142 game-test checks, a `-PtestJade` compatibility run.
- **Broken:** nothing known. The textures and icon come from `tools/gen-textures.js`, built from
  the vanilla textures they sit beside; a file in `art/` with the same name replaces one.
- **Next:** Session 5, the rest of the polish: JEI and REI (both on 26.3: JEI 31.12, REI
  26.3.824; what to show is a category per temperable item with its ingot, or just the three
  recipes plus an info page), sounds and particles tuned by feel in a real game, a play test of
  the strike bar's speed and zones (2 s sweep, wide zone 0.12, narrow 0.04, lag window 0.15),
  and a two-player dedicated server run by hand on each loader. The Beyond the Dragon and
  Mythic Metals runs wait until those mods reach 26.3 (Beyond the Dragon's main is on 26.1.2).
  EMI and WTHIT still have no 26.3 build. Ideas to weigh: oil quenching, bows and shields, the
  trip-hammer.
